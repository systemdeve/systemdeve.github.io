Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what Endless Quiz ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used. Contact: [your email address].

What we collect

  • Account details. When you create an account, you give an email address and a display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name and email address. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password.
  • Leaderboard data. Your display name, best score and level reached are stored so they can appear on the leaderboard. Other signed-in players can see your display name and score. Your email address is not shown.
  • On your device. The game saves your language choice and best score in your browser's local storage.

How we use it

We use this data only to sign you in, show the leaderboard, and keep the game working. We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) stores account and leaderboard data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google's ad services. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on a user's visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.

Your choices

  • Watching an ad is always optional. You choose to watch each one.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and leaderboard entry by emailing the address above.
  • You can clear the data saved in your browser at any time through your browser settings.

Children

Endless Quiz is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
