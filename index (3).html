Endless Quiz

Endless Quiz is a free brain game that runs in your browser. You get one question per level, and the levels never end. After every answer, you see the correct answer with a short explanation, so you learn something even when you miss.

Play now (sign-in required)

Create a free account with your email or Google. Accounts keep your best score on the leaderboard.

How it works

  • You start with 3 lives and earn one back every 5 levels.
  • Questions get harder as you climb: more digits, bigger numbers, tougher trivia.
  • Streaks give bonus points, and your best score goes on the leaderboard.
  • The game is available in English, Filipino and Spanish.
  • If you run out of lives, you can choose to watch one ad to continue with 1 extra life. Watching is always optional.

What you can practise

  • Mental math: quick arithmetic that scales with your level.
  • Number patterns: spot whether a sequence adds, multiplies, squares, or follows a Fibonacci rule.
  • Binary and Roman numerals: convert and read them, with a step-by-step explanation.
  • Percentages and primes: find a percentage of a number, or pick the prime.
  • Trivia: science, geography, history and more.

Sample questions

Which of these numbers is prime? 21, 27, 31, 33
Answer: 31. The others divide evenly by 3 (21 = 3 × 7, 27 = 3 × 9, 33 = 3 × 11).

What is the binary number 1101 in decimal?
Answer: 13. Add the place values where there is a 1: 8 + 4 + 1 = 13.

What comes next? 3, 6, 12, 24, ?
Answer: 48. Each term is multiplied by 2.

Which planet is the hottest in the solar system?
Answer: Venus. Its thick CO2 atmosphere traps heat, hotter than even Mercury.
